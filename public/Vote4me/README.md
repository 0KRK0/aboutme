# /Vote4me

Self-hosted first-person walk around McEwan Hall, Bristo Square and the Informatics Forum,
rendered with three.js (loaded from jsDelivr). No map keys or third-party 3D services.

- `world.txt`, `terrain.txt`: buildings, streets, trees and street furniture from OpenStreetMap,
  terrain from AWS Terrain Tiles (base64 of gzip).
- `env.txt`, `sky.jpg`: sky lighting (Poly Haven, CC0).
- `tex/`: photo-scanned materials (Poly Haven, CC0).
- `hero_*.jpg`: Wikimedia Commons photographs (CC BY-SA) projected onto McEwan Hall, Teviot Row
  House and the Informatics Forum; `*_m.jpg` are their masks.
- `img/`: campaign posters and the ballot QR code.
